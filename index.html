CyberPrint

Fast, simple document printing

1. Request access

Enter your details. The administrator will give you a one-time password (OTP).

2. Verify OTP

Enter the six-digit OTP provided by the administrator. It expires after five minutes.

3. Upload documents

You can add several files. Maximum 20 MB per file. Accepted formats include PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, JPG, PNG, WEBP and TXT.
